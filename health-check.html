How well does your organisation think?

The Thinking Smarter Health Check

How well does your organisation see the bigger picture? Take this free, five-minute assessment to explore how your organisation thinks, learns and responds to challenges. Rate 16 statements about your organisation and 4 about your own practices, note the learning tools it uses, and get your own results dashboard with practical suggestions.

Doing this with your team? Run a Team Health Check live in a workshop or an informal team session, and see everyone's combined results.

Anonymous: we never ask for your name or contact details. Privacy note

Part A

Where your organisation is now

Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), thinking about your organisation as a whole.

Part B

Your own practices

Now think about yourself. Rate each statement from 1 (strongly disagree) to 5 (strongly agree), based on what you usually do at work.

Part C

Your learning tools

Which of these does your organisation use to learn and improve? Tick all that apply, then rate how effective each one is, from 1 (not effective) to 5 (very effective).

Optional

About your organisation

These help us understand how different organisations learn. You can leave any of them blank.

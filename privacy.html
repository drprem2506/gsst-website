Privacy note

This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we never ask for your name or contact details unless you choose to email us.

The Systems Thinking Health Check

Your results are worked out in your own browser. Your latest scores are also kept in your browser, so you can compare them if you take the check again; you can clear them by clearing your browser's site data.

If you leave Share my anonymous answers ticked, we receive your 16 ratings, the learning tools you selected and their ratings (including the name of any tool you add under Other), your optional sector, organisation size and level, and the date. We do not receive your name, email address, IP address or any other contact details, and we cannot link your answers to you. If you untick the box, none of your answers are sent.

We also count, anonymously, how many people start and finish the Health Check. Each count records only the date.

How we use this information

We use it to understand how organisations learn and to improve our programmes. Once enough organisations have taken part, the Health Check shows average scores so you can compare. If we publish findings, we only report groups of five or more responses.

Anonymous answers are stored in a private Google Sheet held by The Global School of Systems Thinking.

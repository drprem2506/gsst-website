If nothing changes

Enter some figures you track over time. See where they are heading if things carry on as they are, which results may be misleading, and what one small change could do.

1. Start with the facts

Choose something you can count or measure, so it can go up or down. Try one:

For example 100 for test results in percent.

Estimates are a useful start, but memory tends to exaggerate recent and dramatic events. Check against records where you can.

At least 6, separated by commas or spaces.

For example: issues that are never reported, or figures from one team only.

3. Try a small change

How much the change shifts the figures once it is fully working.

Most changes take time before they show in the figures.

4. Keep your record

Come back each week, month or test, add your latest figure to the end, and watch the pattern build. For a team, repeat the Team Health Check every few months and track its scores here.

Your privacy

What you enter is saved only in this browser, on this device. GSST does not collect or track it, and nothing is sent to us.

To keep a copy, or to use another device, download your figures and open the file there. Clearing your browser history may remove what is saved. On a shared computer, others using this browser could see it, so use Clear my figures when you are done.

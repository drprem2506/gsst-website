Run a Team Health Check

See how your team thinks together, and talk about it. Everyone answers anonymously on their phone; you see the combined results.

Use it live in your own workshops, or in an informal team session such as a team meeting or a coffee catch-up. You run it yourself; no facilitator or sign-up is needed.

Why do it as a team?

See the differences

One person's view shows one part of the picture. Together, you see where people agree and, more usefully, where they see things differently, and why.

Hear every voice

Because answers are anonymous, people can say what they really think, including those who usually stay quiet or are newest to the team.

Talk about how you work

The results turn the conversation from who is to blame to what in the way you work keeps causing problems.

Build a shared language

The four areas give the team simple words for patterns, causes, beliefs and changing conditions, which you can keep using after the session.

Agree one step together

The session ends with one small thing the team will try in the next month, so the discussion leads to action.

Track progress

Run it again in a few months with the same team to see what has changed.

Before you start: three ground rules

  1. There are no right answers. Different views are the most useful part of the discussion.
  2. Talk about the system, not the people. Ask “what in how we work leads to this?”, not “whose fault is it?”
  3. Don’t try to guess who answered what. It is natural to wonder, but second-guessing makes people less honest, and honest answers are what lead to real improvements. The check is built so that no one, including the person running it, can tell: results appear only once at least three people have answered, and new answers are added in groups.

Works best for groups of up to 30 people. For larger groups, run a separate session for each team or table.

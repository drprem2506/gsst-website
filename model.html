Every system creates a story

The GSST Thinking Smarter Model

Five questions that take you from what happened to where one small change could make the biggest difference. Start with the story, and follow the map round. Click any part to find out more.

The systems that shape our choices are often hidden from view. The model helps make them visible, one question at a time.

Thinking smarter is a discipline, built through four practices: notice patterns, see connections, question beliefs and test small changes.

The arrows run round the map: each new story starts the cycle again.

Going deeper, together

The map is where the learning begins

In our facilitated sessions, participants apply the Thinking Smarter Model to real issues from their own organisation. Working together, they often discover gaps that are hard to see alone:

  • What we observeThe patterns a team misses because it is too close to them.
  • What we know, and don't knowThe assumptions and blind spots a group shares without realising it.
  • How we actThe distance between what we intend and what we actually do.

These gaps are where the most valuable learning happens.
